Documented Failure Case Analysis (results/failure_case.md)

In prompt 2 ("upbeat workout electronic"), prompt expansion degraded fidelity by -0.0654 CLAP cosine. The root cause is Codebook Saturation: Meta's 300M-parameter MusicGen small employs an EnCodec residual vector quantizer with only 4 codebooks at 50 Hz. When an LLM adds heavy bass, pumping kicks, bright arpeggiated synths, and driving snares simultaneously, the model's 300M parameters run out of codebook bandwidth to represent every instrument sharply.
